A restaurant may also be closed if it scores 28 or more points on three consecutive inspections.
A score of 0-13 results in a grade of A.
A score of 14-27 results in a grade of B.
A score of 28 or more results in a grade of C.
Every food service establishment is scheduled for at least one inspection per year.
A restaurant that scores 0 to 13 violations points on its first inspection will receive an A-grade card that must be posted immediately.
If an A is issued on re-inspection, the A-grade card must be posted immediately.
An establishment receiving a B or C grade on re-inspection receives two cards: one showing the letter grade and one that says "Grade Pending"; one of those cards must be posted immediately.
Restaurants with A grades are inspected less often than those with B or C grades.
