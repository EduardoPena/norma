  \caption{%
    End-to-end evaluation of \textsc{Norma} against two baselines across four datasets.
    \textsc{Norma} extracts integrity constraints from regulatory text and validates them
    against a target dataset; results are shown for four pipeline variants that differ in
    how sentences are selected and how constraints are mapped.
    \textbf{XiYan-SQL} is a Text-to-SQL baseline that generates SQL queries directly from
    regulatory sentences and uses their results as violation evidence.
    \textbf{Profiling} is a data-driven DC-Discovery baseline that requires no regulatory
    text: we ran a grid search over top-$k$ ($k \in \{20, 50\}$), ranking strategies, and
    exactness thresholds, and report the best-performing configuration under exact discovery
    and under approximate discovery separately.
    The table is split into two evaluation scopes.
    \textit{All sentences} (left): metrics computed over every sentence in the document,
    including \textbf{Rule Detection} (does the pipeline identify constraint-bearing sentences?)
    and \textbf{Violation Discovery (Aggr.)} (row-level violation overlap pooled across all
    constraints). \textbf{Time} reports total pipeline wall-clock time in seconds.
    \textit{GT sentences only} (right): metrics restricted to sentences carrying a
    ground-truth constraint.
    \textbf{Constraint Extraction} measures exact set-membership of predicted vs.\ gold
    predicate sets after normalisation.
    \textbf{Violation Discovery (Pair.)} reports per-sentence average P/R/F$_1$ against
    the gold violation set.
    n/a indicates the metric is not applicable for the given variant.
    Bold marks the best result per metric and dataset.%
  }
