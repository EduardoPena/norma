  \caption{%
    Results of the constraint extraction pipeline evaluated on two regulatory datasets.
    The table is divided into two evaluation scopes.
    \textit{All sentences} (left): metrics computed over every sentence in the document.
    \textbf{Rule Detection} treats the mapper as an implicit classifier---a sentence
    is predicted positive if the mapper produces any constraint for it.
    \textbf{Violation Discovery (Aggr.)} pools all predicted and all gold constraints
    and measures row-level violation overlap at the dataset level (precision, recall, F\textsubscript{1}).
    \textit{GT sentences only} (right): metrics restricted to sentences
    that carry a ground-truth constraint.
    \textbf{Constraint Extraction} measures exact set-membership of predicted
    vs.\ gold predicate sets after normalisation.
    \textbf{Violation Discovery (Pair.)} measures, per GT sentence, whether
    the predicted constraint identifies the same data violations as the gold constraint
    (average precision, recall, F\textsubscript{1} across sentences).
    \textbf{Time} reports total pipeline runtime in seconds.
    n/a indicates the metric is not applicable for this variant.
    Best result per metric group and dataset in \textbf{bold}.%
